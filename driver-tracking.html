    Driver Guidance

    Keep the tracking session reliable during a delivery run.

    • Keep this page open while the route is in progress.
    • Allow location permission when your browser asks for it.
    • Battery saver modes can reduce GPS frequency or stop updates in the background.
    • Some mobile browsers limit background location sharing when the screen is locked.
    • Stay online when possible so dispatch can see current driver status without delay.
