Phone tracking ·

Live position comes from the free Traccar Client app on the driver's Android phone. It keeps sending while Google Maps is navigating and the screen is locked.

Set up the driver's phone
  1. Install Traccar Client from the Play Store.
  2. In the app, enter the Device identifier and Server URL above.
  3. Location accuracy: High. Interval: 30 seconds. Distance: 0. Stationary heartbeat: 300 seconds.
  4. Turn on Offline buffering and Wake lock.
  5. Allow location All the time.
  6. Phone Settings → Apps → Traccar Client → Battery: Unrestricted. On Xiaomi, OPPO, vivo and Huawei also allow autostart and background activity.
  7. Switch the app's Service status on when the shift starts and off when it ends.

The identifier works like a password: anyone who has it can report this driver's position. If it is shared by mistake, choose Regenerate.
