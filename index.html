I’ve never planned a route. Can I use GoRouteX?

Yes. Choose your stops, review the suggested driving order and check the map. Clear steps help you build your first plan.

What does the free Basic plan include?

Basic includes up to 50 saved stops, 8 stops per route and 1 saved route a day. Saved stops and routes use device-only storage.

Can I import orders?

Yes. Order Hub supports spreadsheet imports and manual orders. Review the details before saving, then choose ready orders for route planning.

Can I share updates through WhatsApp?

Yes. Open a prepared ETA or unable-to-visit message, review it and send it through WhatsApp.

What should I check before setting off?

Check the addresses, stop order and map. Travel times are estimates, and road conditions can change.

Do I need to install an app?

No. GoRouteX runs in your browser on desktop or mobile.
