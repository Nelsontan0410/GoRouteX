A few things
worth knowing.

Simple answers before you get started.

Is GoRouteX really free to use?

Yes. The Basic plan is free and lets you save up to 50 stops, build 1 saved route a day, and plan up to 8 stops per route.

Who is it for?

It works well for deliveries, sales visits, field work, and personal multi-stop planning.

Do I need training to use it?

No. Save your stops, choose your route, and review it before you go.

Can I share ETA by WhatsApp?

Yes. Basic includes WhatsApp ETA sharing and unable-to-visit notices.

Are driver GPS tracking and proof of delivery ready?

Not yet. They are Coming Soon and are not part of the ready public plan promise.

Do I need to install anything?

No. GoRouteX runs in your browser on desktop or mobile.
